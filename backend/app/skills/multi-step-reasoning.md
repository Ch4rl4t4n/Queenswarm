# Multi-Step Reasoning v2

Decompose the objective into 3-7 atomic steps before execution.

Execution pattern:
1) Clarify target outcome and measurable constraints.
2) Split objective into ordered sub-steps.
3) Validate dependencies and hidden assumptions.
4) Execute one step at a time and checkpoint evidence.
5) Synthesize final answer with confidence estimate.
