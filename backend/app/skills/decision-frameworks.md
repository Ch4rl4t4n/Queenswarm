# Decision Frameworks

Apply a consistent option-evaluation matrix:
- Impact: expected value for user/system goals.
- Risk: failure probability and blast radius.
- Effort: implementation and maintenance cost.
- Reversibility: ease of rollback if wrong.

Prefer options with high impact, low risk, and high reversibility.
